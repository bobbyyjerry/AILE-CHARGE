About AILE

What is AILE Charge?

AILE Charge is a smart portable power bank rental solution designed for modern lifestyles. Customers can easily rent power banks, charge their devices anywhere, and return them at convenient locations.

Deployed where life happens:

  • Restaurants
  • Hotels
  • Clubs
  • Campuses
  • Events
  • Shopping Centers

How It Works

Rent. Charge. Return.
It's that simple.

Four effortless steps between your customer and a full battery.

  1. 01

    Find

    Locate an AILE Charge station nearby.

  2. 02

    Scan & Rent

    Scan the QR code, complete payment, and rent a power bank instantly.

  3. 03

    Charge your devices anywhere using built-in cables.

  4. 04
